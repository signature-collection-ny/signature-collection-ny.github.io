Questions

Before you ask

Who can buy from Signature?

Beauty supply stores and other retailers with an approved vendor account. We are wholesale only and do not sell to the public.

How do I see wholesale prices?

Styles and colors are open to everyone. Prices appear after you sign in to the catalog with your business name and vendor code.

Are the wigs human hair?

No. Signature Collection is a synthetic line, including heat-friendly fiber, HD lace and glueless lace front styles.
